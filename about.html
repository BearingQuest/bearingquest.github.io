About

Bearing Quest is my personal project family: a set of open-source tools, the BearingQuest GitHub organization, and this site. It isn't a business.

I'm Dusten Harrison, CISSP, an engineering leader with 20+ years across site reliability, platform engineering, cloud infrastructure, DevOps and cybersecurity. I've led distributed teams across the Americas, Europe and Asia, owned production reliability and vulnerability programs, and worked closely with security and compliance partners on cost and risk.

These projects are how I keep learning in the open. I specify and direct each one; the implementation is AI-generated under my review. The governance calls are mine: human approval gates before anything publishes or merges, permission limits on AI coding agents, frozen acceptance tests, supply-chain checks, and release provenance.

I also mentor through ADPList and MentorCruise, and write about governing AI coding agents on my blog, Signal Over Noise.

The best way to reach me is LinkedIn.
